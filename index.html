Privacy Policy — Sahar

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Sahar — Prayer Times & Adhan ("Sahar", "the app") is built on a simple promise: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your location never goes to us, the developer.

What the app collects

The app does not create an account for you, does not track you, does not show ads, and does not send analytics or crash reports to us. The only personal information that ever leaves your phone is your location, and only for Nearby Mosques — when you open it, and when the app warms the mosque list at startup from your last-known location (see Location below).

Location

Sahar uses your location, with your permission, to show prayer times for your city and the direction of the Qibla. Prayer times are calculated on your device — your coordinates are not sent to us.

Your city name is looked up on your device from a list built into the app — your coordinates are not sent anywhere for this.

Nearby Mosques sends your location to third-party services:

  • Nearby Mosques: when you open Nearby Mosques, your location is sent to Apple (MapKit) and to the Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) to fetch nearby mosque listings. These are real-time requests. The app also warms the mosque list at startup from your last-known location, through the same two services, so the list is ready when you open it. Mosque results from Apple are kept in memory only for your current session and are never saved to your phone. OpenStreetMap results may be cached on your phone for up to 30 days so the list still works offline — the cache lives on your device only and is never sent to us.

Your location is never sent to us, the developer, and we never store it.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No prayer times or personal information leave your phone for notifications.

Purchases

If you make a purchase in the app, payment is processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment information.

Support

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and message and use them only to answer you. That correspondence is the only personal information we ever receive, and it comes from you directly — never from the app.

Apple-provided information

Apple may show us anonymous, aggregate download totals in App Store Connect. If you enable "Share iPhone Analytics" and "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone Settings, Apple may share crash reports with us. Both are controlled by Apple and your device settings — the app itself sends us nothing.

Children's privacy

Sahar is a general-audience prayer utility. The app never asks for your age, name, or any information, and it has no accounts — so there is no children's data to protect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
